Worldbuilding Review and Design-Readiness Plan

Purpose

This review captures the current state of the Neon Champions wiki after the May 2026 expansion, identifies the most interesting material, names the loose connections, and proposes the next design-ready development pass.

It is a planning/audit page, not canon.

What Is Strong and Interesting

1. Maintenance as power

The strongest setting engine is that civilization survives through systems it cannot safely turn off: white-sky, agi-compact-and-compute-deterrence, great-corporate-persons, biotech-and-mutation, synthetic-reality-and-polluted-feeds, echo-identity-and-death, and world-map-and-political-economy.

This is better than generic megacorp dystopia. The horror is dependency, liability, auditability, uptime, and continuity.

2. White Sky is a literal and political image

white-sky works because it is visible before it is metaphorical. Everyone lives under the same altered sky, but not everyone receives the same safety, cooling, insurance, rainfall, clean feed, or adaptation body.

The best extensions are not more climate theory. They are concrete: Meridian Stations, deployment geography, maintenance rituals, sacrificial regions, blue-sky breaks, and everyday sky politics.

3. The Great Corporate Persons have a distinct organizing principle

The current roster is compelling because each entity controls a continuity layer rather than a product category:

Frontier compute / lawful cognitionhelvetia

This gives the world strategic sponsors without making every faction a corporation.

4. Baltic War → Echoes → Vezha is emotionally grounded

The baltic-war now does useful work: it explains European security trauma, Ukrainian doctrine, Vezha Systems, kantograd, and the origin of Memorial Echoes.

Echoes are strongest when they begin as grief technology, not as a clean immortality product.

5. Champions have strong social charge

champions and champion-worship connect transhuman bodies, AI anxiety, celebrity, myth, strong-leader politics, and strategic command. chorus-exchange gives Champion fame real infrastructure.

The risk is that Champion still means too many things.

Main Gaps

1. Design bridges are missing

The wiki has lore pages and raw brainstorms, but almost no actual design-bridges/ pages. The first bridge set should be:

  1. design-bridges/champions.md
  2. design-bridges/white-sky-and-meridian-stations.md
  3. design-bridges/digital-net-and-polluted-feed.md
  4. design-bridges/body-subscriptions-and-mutation.md
  5. design-bridges/great-corporate-persons.md
  6. design-bridges/world-map-and-campaign-crisis.md

2. Factions are still axes, not factions

factions-as-evolutionary-philosophies is useful, but the wiki needs actual faction seed pages. The first six should probably be:

  • Sky Loyalists / Stabilization Citizens
  • Blue Restorationists / Open Sky cells
  • Body Sovereigns
  • Free-Net Archivists
  • Human-Exceptionalist Champion Leagues
  • Machine-Emergence / VI-Acceleration sects

Each faction needs worldview, territory/power base, body philosophy, Digital-Net posture, Champion posture, heroic version, horrific version, visuals, and strategic hooks.

3. Champions need boundaries

Needed constraints:

  • who can certify or deny Champion status;
  • rarity by region;
  • difference between celebrity, elite operator, altered person, and Champion;
  • required costs and maintenance;
  • false / failed / illegal / retired / Echo Champions;
  • why armies and publics follow Champions despite drones, VI, and professional officers.

4. Digital-Net needs architecture

digital-net is a central mystery but not yet a concrete network. It needs layers:

  • public feed layer;
  • corporate service layer;
  • municipal sensor layer;
  • battlefield mesh;
  • Axiom-certified record layer;
  • Chorus attention/payment layer;
  • Helvetia lawful-compute layer;
  • Free-Net fragments;
  • dead archives;
  • Echo-contaminated zones;
  • hostile model farms.

Each layer should have access methods, dangers, class markers, typical users, and game hooks.

5. Everyday life is thin

The setting has strong macro institutions but needs lived texture:

  • commute under polluted overlays;
  • cheap blue-sky subscriptions;
  • body-plan downgrades;
  • clinic waiting rooms;
  • Echo mourning;
  • food-continuity ration thresholds;
  • Champion fan economies;
  • Axiom proof rituals;
  • Chorus greylisting;
  • Free-Net archive raids.

6. Timeline spine is not yet settled

The wiki needs a single timeline that aligns:

  • Baltic War / Vistula-Daugava War;
  • Memorial Echo origins;
  • White Sky deployment;
  • UNP formation;
  • AGI Compact formalization;
  • Great Corporate Person recognition;
  • Blue Week.

Without this, the setting is coherent thematically but slightly floating historically.

Loose Connections to Tie Together

White Sky + Chorus

Chorus should not only monetize Champions. It should monetize sky perception: blue-sky overlays, climate dashboards, verified outrage, disaster streams, restoration martyrdom, and Sky Loyalist reassurance.

Axiom + Echoes + AGI Compact

Axiom decides identity/admissibility; Helvetia decides lawful cognition; Echoes sit at the boundary. A dedicated page should explain why advanced Echoes are not automatically forbidden AGI, or why some factions think they are.

Verdant + Janus-Kestrel + World Map

Food may exist but fail to arrive or fail to become administratively recognized as famine. Verdant controls feedability; Janus-Kestrel controls movement; Axiom controls records; Chorus controls attention. This is a strong campaign-crisis machine.

Vezha + Champions

Vezha’s war-OS doctrine can explain why Champions matter militarily: some Champions may be human command/legitimacy/coordination nodes who can operate inside degraded, jammed, politically constrained battlefields where pure VI command is illegal or distrusted.

Blue Week + Factions

blue-week should create at least three faction myths: proof the sky can return, proof sabotage can kill, and proof QXZ/Axiom/Chorus can hide or distort planetary truth.

Contradictions / Tensions to Resolve or Preserve

  1. QXZ overlaps with Verdant and future Vitae. Boundary: QXZ owns exposure and adaptation eligibility; Verdant owns food continuity; Vitae owns biological uptime.
  2. Great Corporate Persons currently mix accepted seats and candidates. Clean roster needed after Vitae and Janus-Kestrel are promoted.
  3. “The Eight” remains as an alias even though the roster is now nine. Decide whether it is deprecated, historical, or public shorthand.
  4. Blue-sky breaks need physical rules: regional thinning, aerosol collapse, feed correction, counter-chemistry, or sabotage.
  5. Echoes need a legal boundary under the AGI Compact.

Recommended Development Order

Phase 1: Wiki and publication hygiene

  • Publish all current material, including raw/brainstorms/ and raw/references/.
  • Fix public Quartz base URL to myriwe-bot.github.io/neon-champions-world-site.
  • Keep index/log/schema current.
  • Run link/tag/page-count checks after publication.

Phase 2: Promote missing megacorp seats

  • Create organizations/vitae.md.
  • Create organizations/janus-kestrel.md.
  • Rewrite great-corporate-persons into a clean nine-seat roster table.
  • Update united-nations-and-persons to reference all nine.

Phase 3: Build the playable faction layer

Create the first six faction seed pages listed above. Do not make them final rosters yet; make them strong philosophies with concrete rituals, places, dependencies, and contradictions.

Phase 4: Make Champions design-legible

  • Rewrite champions with constraints, types, costs, certification, failure modes, and examples.
  • Expand champion-worship into subcultures.
  • Create design-bridges/champions.md.

Phase 5: Add core design bridges

Create the six design bridge pages listed in Main Gaps. These are the bridge from lore wiki to game design/GDD.

Phase 6: Add timeline and everyday life

  • Create canon/timeline/setting-timeline.md.
  • Create cultures/everyday-life-under-white-sky.md.
  • Add a handful of short in-world scenes: clinic, feed, commute, food queue, Echo mourning, Champion event.

Superpowers Gate Questions

Use these to decide the next implementation/design plan. Answer one at a time, starting with the first.

  1. Should the next pass prioritize design-readiness or lore concreteness?

    • A: Design-readiness: create design bridges first.
    • B: Lore concreteness: factions, everyday life, and timeline first.
    • C: Megacorp completion: Vitae, Janus-Kestrel, and roster cleanup first.

  2. Should the Great Corporate Person roster now be treated as exactly nine, or as the current nine with future political churn?

  3. Should raw brainstorm and reference pages remain publicly published by default? Current direction from this task: yes.

  4. What should Champions primarily become for design purposes?

    • hero/commander units;
    • faction leaders;
    • strategic propaganda assets;
    • rare map entities;
    • all of the above with strict constraints.

  5. Which first faction should be developed deeply enough to become a playable prototype reference?
