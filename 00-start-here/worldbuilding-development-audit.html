Worldbuilding Development Audit

Purpose

This page tracks structural problems in the wiki and the next work needed to make Neon Champions feel concrete rather than abstract.

Current Sanity Rules

  1. Canon and world-rule pages should read primarily as in-universe setting material.
  2. Real-world research belongs in raw/references/, trope studies, or explicit design/reference pages, not inside canon pages as citations or report summaries.
  3. A page should stay about its subject. Neighboring systems should be linked and summarized only where they directly touch the subject.
  4. Design/gameplay implications should move into design bridges when they become detailed.
  5. Avoid pages that only say “this could be many things.” Ambiguity is good, but each page needs concrete objects, institutions, rituals, slang, places, costs, and conflicts.

Completed Fix: White Sky

The white-sky page was overcrowded and too out-of-universe. It has been revised to:

  • remove direct real-world report framing from the canon article;
  • keep the science as in-world behavior: a maintained reflective sky, cloud-brightening fleets, sensors, and climate models;
  • cut detailed body-subscription, polluted-feed, VI-governance, and gameplay sections that belong on neighboring pages;
  • emphasize the visible sky first;
  • preserve the corporate-first-mover origin without naming the corporation yet;
  • keep real-world scientific background in raw/references/national-academies-albedo-modification-chapter-2.md.

Wider Wiki Findings

1. Canon pages still contain too much agent-facing language

Several pages contain phrases like “should,” “possible,” “potential design bridge hooks,” “do not answer yet,” or “seed concept based on user direction.” This is useful working material but not ideal for canon pages.

Affected pages:

  • champions
  • digital-net
  • echo-identity-and-death
  • virtual-intelligence-and-compute-scarcity
  • biotech-and-mutation
  • synthetic-reality-and-polluted-feeds
  • factions-as-evolutionary-philosophies

Recommended fix: keep canon pages in-universe, then move design pressure and authorial warnings into design bridges or this audit page.

2. The wiki has pillars but not enough concrete institutions

The world has strong pressures: white-sky, digital-net, biotech-and-mutation, synthetic-reality-and-polluted-feeds, virtual-intelligence-and-compute-scarcity, champions.

What is missing is the layer that makes those pressures tangible:

  • named corporations;
  • named cities and regions;
  • named maintenance fleets, clinics, networks, cults, militias, insurers, and regulators;
  • everyday jobs and rituals;
  • laws, scandals, disasters, slogans, brands, and public holidays;
  • concrete examples of how ordinary people live inside the systems.

3. White Sky needs named ownership

The strongest next White Sky work is not more climate theory. It is concreteness:

  • the corporation that first touched the sky;
  • what it sold before deployment;
  • what assets it was protecting;
  • what legal fiction let it act;
  • what its logo, slogan, public memorials, and hated nicknames are;
  • which regions gained safety and which paid the cost;
  • what its successor institutions look like now.

Candidate page to create next: organizations/[white-sky-corporation].md once the corporation direction is chosen.

4. Factions are still mostly axes, not factions

factions-as-evolutionary-philosophies is a good constraint page, but the setting now needs 4-6 actual faction seeds with:

  • worldview;
  • territory or power base;
  • relationship to White Sky;
  • relationship to Champions;
  • body philosophy;
  • Digital-Net posture;
  • heroic and horrific version;
  • concrete units/visuals later.

Suggested first faction seeds:

  • Sky Loyalist corporate citizens / stabilization defenders.
  • Blue Restorationist insurgents or restoration cults.
  • Body-sovereignty movements against subscription flesh.
  • Free-net archivists who treat old climate truth as territory.
  • Human-exceptionalist Champion cults or leagues.
  • Machine-emergence / VI-acceleration sects.

5. Champions need boundaries

champions is thematically strong but still too broad. It needs constraints so Championhood does not mean “anything important.”

Develop next:

  • how many Champions exist in a region;
  • who can legally certify one;
  • what a false Champion is;
  • what a failed Champion is;
  • what costs every Champion must pay;
  • why armies follow them instead of simply using drones, VI, or ordinary officers.

6. Digital-Net needs concrete layers

digital-net works as a mystery but needs architecture:

  • public feed layer;
  • corporate service layer;
  • municipal sensor layer;
  • battlefield mesh;
  • free-net fragments;
  • dead archives;
  • hostile model farms;
  • Echo-contaminated zones.

Each layer should have access methods, dangers, social class markers, and typical users.

7. Biotech needs products and failure modes

biotech-and-mutation is clear thematically but needs examples:

  • three mass-market body subscriptions;
  • one elite body platform;
  • one black-clinic mutation practice;
  • one climate-zone license;
  • one repossession horror story;
  • one faction that treats mutation as sacred rather than shameful.

8. Synthetic reality needs everyday scenes

synthetic-reality-and-polluted-feeds needs lived texture:

  • what a normal commute looks like;
  • what a cheap fake blue sky looks like during a brownout;
  • what a feed scam feels like;
  • how a climate dashboard lies without being fully false;
  • what “clean feed” costs;
  • how someone proves an image, memory, or weather reading is real.

Highest-Value Next Pages

  1. organizations/[white-sky-corporation].md — the corporation that initiated the White Sky.
  2. locations/[first-white-sky-test-region].md — the place where the first irreversible deployment happened.
  3. canon/history/white-sky-deployment.md — a contested timeline of deployment, denial, acceptance, and dependency.
  4. factions/blue-restorationists.md — first concrete anti-White-Sky faction.
  5. factions/sky-loyalists.md — first concrete pro-maintenance faction.
  6. technologies/body-subscription-platforms.md — concrete biotech products and ownership regimes.
  7. canon/world-rules/digital-net-layers.md — concrete Digital-Net architecture.
  8. cultures/everyday-life-under-white-sky.md — ordinary lived reality.

Recommended Rewrite Order

  1. Finish White Sky concreteness: corporation, event, regions.
  2. Create 2 opposed factions around White Sky.
  3. Create one everyday-life page to ground tone.
  4. Rewrite Champions with tighter constraints.
  5. Split design/gameplay notes from canon pages into design bridges.
  6. Run another link and index audit.
