United Nations and Persons

One-Line Summary

The United Nations and Persons is the post-crisis successor to the old UN order: a weak world-government substitute but powerful crisis, inspection, sanctions, and legitimacy forum that recognizes both nations and a tiny class of systemically necessary corporate persons.

Canon Status

Reviewed soft-canon. The UNP formula is accepted for now: it is the post-crisis charter institution that makes the AGI/ASI prohibition, White Sky maintenance duties, corporate recognition, and low-intensity conflict logic legible without becoming a true world government.

What It Is

The UNP is an international charter institution built after the old state-only order failed to manage overlapping crises: climate emergency, White Sky disputes, AI threshold scares, infrastructure collapse, post-Baltic-War security realignment, cable and food shocks, migration pressure, and corporate control of essential systems.

It did not abolish states. It did not end regional blocs. It did not create a world parliament with command over humanity.

It did something more cyberpunk and more plausible: it formalized the fact that some non-state actors had become too necessary and too dangerous to leave outside the room.

The Meaning of Persons

“Persons” is the institution’s most contested word.

It can mean:

  • natural human persons whose survival and rights the charter claims to protect;
  • juridical persons such as corporations, foundations, trusts, and treaty vehicles;
  • collective persons such as city compacts, labor federations, indigenous authorities, and recognized polities;
  • disputed future persons such as Echo estates or continuity shells, which the UNP carefully avoids fully recognizing.

Supporters call the name a moral expansion beyond state sovereignty. Critics call it the moment corporate personhood swallowed the world.

Origin

The old UN did not need to be ceremonially destroyed. It became insufficient.

The likely sequence:

  1. The Security Council repeatedly failed during climate and security crises.
  2. Emergency side compacts formed around climate continuity, frontier compute, food logistics, cable security, migration corridors, and private military restraint.
  3. Megacorps were first invited as technical operators, then as signatories, then as bound parties, then as recognized persons.
  4. States realized that any serious AGI, climate, or infrastructure compact without QXZ-scale and Vezha-scale actors was theater.
  5. The UNP Charter consolidated these side arrangements into a new institutional shell.

The result is not a clean triumph of global governance. It is a treaty around dependency.

The Charter Bargain

The UNP Charter rests on several bargains.

AGI / ASI Restraint

The charter formalizes the AGI Compact. No signatory may develop, deploy, transfer, or operate unsupervised AGI or ASI above recognized thresholds. Frontier compute is declared, licensed, evaluated, and inspected.

Climate Continuity

White Sky and other climate-maintenance systems cannot be abruptly abandoned or sabotaged without triggering charter sanctions. This protects billions and also protects the corporations that made planetary maintenance proprietary.

Anti-Conquest Norm

Large-scale territorial conquest is forbidden. Formal annexation by great powers becomes rare. This does not prevent proxy wars, corridor wars, receivership coups, concession wars, or private-security campaigns.

Recognition for Duties

The Great Corporate Persons receive seats, procedural rights, and legitimacy because they accept declared duties: infrastructure continuity, inspection, disclosure, emergency response, and sanctions compliance.

Continuity Council

The UNP’s most important body is usually called the Continuity Council.

It is not exactly the old Security Council. It is a crisis forum for existential infrastructure.

Its powers include:

  • defining AGI/ASI threshold violations;
  • authorizing compute inspections and model-weight custody orders;
  • coordinating sanctions against rogue labs or noncompliant states;
  • classifying protected continuity infrastructure;
  • mediating White Sky maintenance crises;
  • reviewing large territorial-war violations;
  • declaring infrastructure emergency zones;
  • coordinating de-escalation between major blocs and corporate persons.

Its weakness is that it relies on states and megacorps to enforce decisions. Its strength is that almost everyone needs its certifications, insurance recognition, procurement legitimacy, and crisis language.

Corporate Seats

Corporate seats are not ordinary sovereign seats.

In the cleaner legal theory, recognized corporations are seated only for domains where they control systemic infrastructure. In practice, those domains include nearly everything that matters.

A corporate person may vote or hold formal standing on:

  • AGI/compute threshold compliance;
  • infrastructure continuity;
  • sanctions that affect declared systems;
  • emergency maintenance corridors;
  • climate, food, body, identity, and communications continuity.

They are more constrained on pure territorial sovereignty questions, but this line is constantly litigated because infrastructure and territory cannot be cleanly separated.

What It Actually Does

The UNP is strongest at:

  • producing legal language for crisis;
  • certifying legitimacy;
  • coordinating inspections;
  • threatening sanctions;
  • defining what counts as protected infrastructure;
  • laundering ugly bargains into continuity doctrine;
  • giving states and megacorps a room where direct escalation can be walked back.

It is weak at:

  • stopping small wars;
  • protecting sacrificed territories;
  • forcing megacorps to share technology fairly;
  • proving attribution in cyber or model incidents;
  • preventing private coercion when it is wrapped in insurance, arbitration, or maintenance necessity.

Relationship to Megacorps

The UNP is the legal home of the Great Corporate Persons.

QXZ Meridian

QXZ Meridian uses UNP climate-continuity language to protect White Sky maintenance and Meridian Stations. Opponents say the UNP legalized QXZ’s hostage system after the fact.

Vezha Systems

Vezha Systems is useful to the UNP because it sells survival under attack: drones, verified feeds, logistics, civil-defense continuity, and limited-war command stacks. Its systems also make proxy war cleaner, faster, and easier to deny.

Barents Research Group

Barents Research Group supplies polar data, Arctic route certification, undersea cable security, cold compute, and extraction-risk models. Its research outputs often become UNP-recognized facts.

Public Face

The UNP presents itself through solemn humanist language:

Nations preserve peace. Persons preserve continuity.

No mind above law. No climate without duty. No war beyond return.

Humanity remains the author of history.

The slogans are not wholly false. That is why they work.

Horrific Version

The horrific version of the UNP is not a single tyrannical world state. It is a permission structure.

It tells the world which infrastructure must be protected, which harms are acceptable side effects, which corporate persons are necessary, which territories are outside continuity guarantees, and which conflicts are small enough not to count as world-threatening.

It can condemn conquest while tolerating receivership. It can ban AGI while preserving compute aristocracy. It can protect climate maintenance while making the White Sky permanent. It can recognize persons while leaving ordinary people trapped in service jurisdictions.

Heroic Version

The heroic version is also real.

Without the UNP, the world might have crossed the AGI threshold, abandoned climate maintenance, escalated the Baltic War aftermath, shattered food logistics, or let corporate infrastructure operate with no treaty obligations at all.

Many honest diplomats, engineers, doctors, labor negotiators, and city officials use the UNP to prevent worse disasters.

Game / Story Uses

  • Source of missions involving inspections, sanctions, data escrow, and emergency corridors.
  • Legitimacy layer behind why megacorps can act like states without fully replacing states.
  • Arena for faction diplomacy and crisis countdowns.
  • Explanation for why open AGI/ASI and large territorial wars are rare while local conflicts are common.
  • Place where Champions can be declared envoys, terrorists, protected persons, illegal weapons, or treaty anomalies.

Open Questions

  • Is United Nations and Persons the formal title or popular shorthand for a longer charter name?
  • Which states were founding signatories, and which signed under duress?
  • Did QXZ receive recognition before or after the White Sky first move?
  • Are there non-corporate Person seats: labor, cities, indigenous polities, universities, or Echo estates?
  • Does the Continuity Council have vetoes, weighted votes, or subject-matter voting?
  • Which region most openly rejects the UNP as corporate feudalism?
