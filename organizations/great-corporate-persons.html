Great Corporate Persons

One-Line Summary

The Great Corporate Persons are the tiny class of megacorps recognized inside the UNP because each controls a continuity layer the world cannot safely abandon, nationalize, or destroy without risking systemic collapse.

Canon Status

Reviewed soft-canon. The formula is accepted for now: roughly nine Great Corporate Persons are recognized because each controls a continuity layer that can sustain or endanger civilization. The roster expanded from eight to nine when Chorus Exchange became necessary as a distinct attention/liquidity layer rather than being folded into Janus-Kestrel.

What They Are

A Great Corporate Person is not merely a large company. It is a corporation, trust-network, treaty vehicle, or holding structure whose infrastructure has become civilizationally load-bearing.

Recognition is not a prize. It is a bargain.

A recognized corporate person receives:

  • formal standing in UNP processes;
  • access to crisis councils;
  • protection for declared continuity infrastructure;
  • sanctions coordination against attacks on its protected systems;
  • arbitration channels;
  • legitimacy in procurement and insurance markets.

In exchange, it accepts:

  • AGI/ASI compact obligations;
  • compute and model inspections where relevant;
  • continuity duties;
  • emergency operating plans;
  • disclosure rituals;
  • sanctions compliance;
  • liability arbitration;
  • charter review of systemically dangerous conduct.

In practice, the exchange favors incumbents. But it also gives the world some leverage over them.

Why There Are So Few

Many corporations are rich. Few are existentially entangled with survival.

A Great Corporate Person must control at least one continuity layer:

  • atmosphere and climate maintenance;
  • frontier compute and model custody;
  • war and civil-defense operating systems;
  • food, seed, nutrient, or famine logistics;
  • body, organ, prosthetic, fertility, or adaptation infrastructure;
  • identity, feed provenance, civil registry, or Echo legality;
  • ports, cables, orbital relays, customs, or global logistics;
  • attention, media reach, payments, remittances, subscriptions, and liquidity access;
  • polar routes, critical minerals, rescue, and climate archives.

The number nine is the current target. It remains small enough for myth and politics, but large enough to give media/finance its own continuity layer rather than collapsing it into logistics or identity.

Recognized / Strong Candidates

QXZ Meridian

QXZ Meridian is the strongest current Great Person candidate. It controls the continuity stack around White Sky maintenance, adaptation biotech, climate-risk insurance, agrigen, liability science, and Meridian Stations.

Continuity layer: atmosphere / adaptation / climate-risk arbitration.

Public claim: it keeps the climate survivable.

Dark claim: it made survival proprietary.

Vezha Systems

Vezha Systems is the accepted Ukrainian-origin war-OS, drone, civil-defense, and resilience megacorp.

Continuity layer: survival under attack / battlefield and city-resilience coordination.

Public claim: it lets smaller powers and cities survive aggression.

Dark claim: it makes permanent limited war administratively efficient.

Barents Research Group

Barents Research Group is the accepted polar research, route-certification, extraction, cable, rescue, and Arctic data megacorp.

Continuity layer: polar routes / critical minerals / northern data and rescue.

Public claim: it makes a changing Arctic navigable and safe.

Dark claim: it turns measurement into ownership.

Helvetia

Helvetia is the accepted frontier compute, model custody, chip attestation, sovereign cloud, and AGI Compact safeguards megacorp.

Continuity layer: lawful cognition / frontier compute custody / model nonproliferation.

Public claim: it prevents threshold catastrophe.

Dark claim: it froze lawful cognition inside incumbent custody.

Strong Candidates Under Review

Axiom

Axiom is the current recommended candidate for the identity, feed-provenance, civil registry, evidence, and Echo-recognition slot. It is the public name of the post-merger Axiom-SOVRAN trust stack; Axiom Tallinn survives as a regional registry-recovery unit, not the parent megacorp brand.

Continuity layer: civil reality / identity / provenance / admissibility.

Public claim: it protects identity, records, evidence, elections, property, humanitarian aid, and media authenticity from synthetic collapse.

Dark claim: it makes truth and personhood administratively revocable.

Verdant

Verdant is the current recommended candidate for the food, nutrient, seed, crop-risk, grain-routing, emergency-ration, fermentation-protein, and nutrient-recovery slot. Its formal style is probably Verdant Covenant Group, but ordinary usage is just Verdant.

Continuity layer: food / nutrient / seed / famine logistics.

Public claim: it prevents famine through resilient seed systems, nutrient circularity, crop-risk intelligence, grain logistics, and emergency food provisioning.

Dark claim: it turned hunger into an administrable market condition.

Additional Strong Candidates / Recent Seats

Some of these are not yet full organization pages; others have now been promoted into the nine-seat roster.

Body and Health Continuity

Recommended leading candidate: Vitae Continuity, documented in megacorp-7-body-continuity-2026-05-22.

Continuity layer: body continuity / organ systems / prosthetics / fertility / workforce viability / biological uptime.

Public claim: No life interrupted.

Dark claim: you do not own your body’s remaining value.

Boundary: QXZ Meridian owns survival conditions and climate exposure; Vitae owns biological uptime after those conditions reach the body.

Food / Nutrient / Seed Logistics

This slot is now represented by the Verdant reviewed soft-canon candidate.

Core risk remains: avoid making it just “Monsanto but bigger.” Its real power should be famine avoidance, calorie insurance, nutrient circularity, ration thresholds, and the failed-harvest protocol.

Identity / Feed Provenance

This slot is now represented by the Axiom reviewed soft-canon candidate.

Core risk remains: do not make it generic social media. Make it the institution that decides what counts as legally real, admissible, recoverable, and recordable.

Ports / Cables / Orbital Logistics

Recommended leading candidate: Janus-Kestrel Continuity Group, documented in megacorp-8-threshold-logistics-2026-05-22 and revised in janus-kestrel-media-finance-nadir-2026-05-22.

Continuity layer: lawful movement / thresholds / customs / port, cable, drone, and orbital interfaces / cargo and signal legitimacy.

Public claim: Continuity is civilization.

Dark claim: nothing moves, arrives, clears, or relays unless Janus-Kestrel recognizes it.

Boundary: Barents Research Group certifies polar frontiers; Janus-Kestrel decides whether goods, people, bandwidth, repairs, and exceptions can circulate through the global system.

Chorus Exchange

Chorus Exchange is the ninth Great Corporate Person: the media, communications, creator-economy, payment, remittance, advertising, subscription, and reputation-market megacorp.

Continuity layer: attention and liquidity continuity.

Public claim: everyone has a voice and a way to be paid.

Dark claim: if Chorus cannot monetize you, you become socially silent and financially thin.

Champion relevance: Chorus is closely tied to Champions because it manufactures, ranks, amplifies, monetizes, and sometimes destroys the publics around them. It can make a Champion into a saint, brand, scandal, martyr, market signal, recruitment funnel, or politically toxic silence.

Boundary: Axiom says whether something is real; Chorus decides whether anyone sees it, funds it, or cares. Helvetia governs lawful cognition; Chorus uses constrained VI persuasion and ranking systems below the forbidden AGI threshold. Janus-Kestrel carries signals and goods; Chorus monetizes and amplifies the publics carried across those channels.

Political Logic

The Great Persons are recognized because they can credibly threaten the order even without making threats.

If QXZ fails, climate maintenance may collapse. If Vezha withdraws, cities and borders may become undefended. If BRG withholds data, Arctic routes, cables, and mineral claims become legally unstable. If Helvetia defects, the AGI compact may fail. If an identity/provenance company lies, elections, casualties, land claims, and Echo personhood can become unknowable. If Verdant withholds continuity status, food can exist in warehouses while hunger remains administratively unrecognized. If Vitae withdraws body continuity, people can remain alive but functionally nonviable. If Janus-Kestrel denies clearance or relay priority, medicine, food, organs, refugees, repair crews, and emergency signals can exist at the gate but fail to arrive. If Chorus withdraws reach or liquidity, a person, movement, Champion, neighborhood, or small economy can remain legally real while becoming socially silent and financially thin.

This is why states hate them, need them, regulate them, subsidize them, and invite them into the room.

Internal Contradiction

The Great Persons are charter-bound because they are too important to leave unregulated.

They are powerful because charter recognition tells the world they are too important to attack.

The same rule that disciplines them also entrenches them.

Public Face

They prefer to say they are not sovereign.

They say they are:

  • continuity providers;
  • chartered persons;
  • infrastructure custodians;
  • emergency partners;
  • accountable operators;
  • responsible innovators;
  • treaty-bound service entities.

Their critics say these are just softer words for private sovereignty.

Game / Story Uses

  • Natural top-level sponsors for factions without making every faction a corporation.
  • Strategic equivalents of great houses, but grounded in infrastructure rather than bloodlines.
  • Campaign-map powers that can shape conflict through service access, not only armies.
  • Sources of Champions as corporate saints, deniable assets, defectors, inspectors, or treaty anomalies.
  • A roster-building frame for the nine largest megacorps.

Open Questions

  • Is the number exactly nine in the charter, or just the present count after Chorus Exchange was added?
  • Are Great Person recognitions permanent, renewable, inheritable, or revocable?
  • Can a state-owned enterprise become a Great Person?
  • Can a city compact, labor federation, university trust, indigenous authority, or Echo estate become a non-corporate Person?
  • Is Verdant Covenant Group the right formal style, or should “Covenant” remain treaty language while the legal parent is even drier?
  • Should the body-continuity slot become Vitae Continuity, or does Continuance Health Combine / SomaDyne fit better?
  • Should the threshold-logistics slot become Janus-Kestrel Continuity Group? Current direction: yes, because Janus gives thresholds while Kestrel gives speed, drones, couriers, relays, and aerial/orbital movement.
  • How openly does Chorus Exchange sponsor Champions versus merely monetizing and ranking every faction’s Champions?
  • Do any Great Persons secretly sponsor accelerationist AGI work while publicly enforcing the compact?
