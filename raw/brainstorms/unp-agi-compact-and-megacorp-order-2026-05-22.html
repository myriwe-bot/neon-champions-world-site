UNP, AGI Compact, and Megacorp Order Brainstorm

Short Recommendation

The idea is strong, but it should not become a clean corporate world government.

The best Neon Champions version is:

After climate, war, AI, and infrastructure crises make the old UN visibly inadequate, states create the United Nations and Persons as a legitimacy and crisis-management forum. The UNP recognizes a tiny class of Great Corporate Persons because they control infrastructure that can destroy or sustain civilization. In exchange, these corporations accept AGI limits, inspection, climate duties, disclosure rituals, and formal crisis obligations. The arrangement prevents direct apocalypse while making lower-level proxy conflict, concession war, and corporate coercion normal.

This preserves the user’s goal:

  • AGI/ASI are out of bounds by compact, not just genre fiat.
  • Megacorps are internationally recognized, but not as cartoon sovereigns.
  • The UNP matters without becoming a world government.
  • Regional blocs, alliances, concessions, and local conflicts remain the real flow of power.
  • Great-power MAD logic pushes conflict downward into proxy wars and low-intensity violence.

Pushback

Do not make the megacorps simply equal to nations in every respect.

That is the weak version. It makes states look foolish and corporations look like fantasy kingdoms with logos.

The stronger version is legal asymmetry:

  • states remain sovereign;
  • megacorps are recognized as persons, not nations;
  • their seats exist because they are systemically dangerous, not morally legitimate;
  • their voting powers are limited by subject matter;
  • they are bound by inspection, disclosure, emergency continuity duties, sanctions obligations, and liability arbitration;
  • everyone argues about whether recognition was necessary realism or formalized corporate feudalism.

The public should not agree that the arrangement is good. Many people should call it surrender.

What Replaced the Old UN

The old UN likely does not vanish overnight. It becomes inadequate through a sequence:

  1. Security Council paralysis during climate emergency, Baltic War aftermath, AI threshold scares, and White Sky disputes.
  2. States create side compacts for climate continuity, compute governance, frontier AI inspections, cable security, and emergency food logistics.
  3. These side compacts increasingly include the corporations that actually operate the relevant systems.
  4. The old UN becomes ceremonial, humanitarian, or legally inherited.
  5. A post-crisis charter consolidates the side compacts into the United Nations and Persons.

The UNP name should sound aspirational and legalistic, not cute. “Persons” has multiple meanings:

  • natural persons: citizens/humans whose rights the charter claims to protect;
  • juridical persons: corporations and foundations;
  • collective persons: city compacts, indigenous authorities, labor federations, universities, recognized polities;
  • contested future persons: Echo estates, continuity shells, or synthetic-person claims that the charter carefully avoids resolving.

This gives the name bite: the institution is built around a fight over who counts.

The New Charter

The UNP Charter should contain high ideals and ugly compromises.

Likely pillars:

  1. Artificial General Intelligence and Superintelligence Nonproliferation

    • No unsupervised AGI/ASI development, deployment, possession, transfer, or self-improvement.
    • Frontier compute and capability thresholds require declaration, licensing, evals, audits, and inspection.
    • Safe virtual intelligence remains legal and broadly available.

  2. Climate Continuity and Anti-Termination Duties

    • White Sky maintenance cannot be abruptly abandoned.
    • Major climate infrastructure must be declared, insured, inspected, and operated under continuity plans.
    • Deliberate climate-maintenance collapse is treated as a crime against persons.

  3. Prohibition on Large-Scale Territorial War

    • Formal conquest and mass annexation are charter violations.
    • This does not stop corridor wars, private-security conflicts, proxy wars, legal coercion, debt receiverships, or sabotage.

  4. Continuity of Persons

    • Human survival, identity records, civil registries, bodies, medical access, and evacuation rights are charter language.
    • In practice, access depends on documentation, feed provenance, insurance, and platform identity.

  5. Systemic Infrastructure Duties

    • Recognized Great Persons must maintain declared infrastructure: compute, climate, food, medical, logistics, security, identity, communications, and data custody.
    • Failure can trigger receivership, sanctions, inspection, or emergency operating authority.

Security Council Equivalent

The UNP should have a council, but it should not be a simple copy of the UN Security Council.

Candidate name: Continuity Council.

Composition idea:

  • major state seats: US/North Atlantic, China, India, European Union / European Security Compact, African Union or rotating African compact, possibly Brazil/Latin American seat, and others depending on final map;
  • Great Corporate Person seats: up to eight systemically recognized megacorps;
  • rotating regional seats;
  • non-voting or limited-vote chambers for labor, cities, indigenous authorities, scientific panels, and civil society;
  • a technical inspection directorate with unusual independence.

The council does not govern the world. It does four things:

  1. defines threshold violations;
  2. authorizes inspections and sanctions;
  3. coordinates crisis de-escalation;
  4. decides what counts as protected continuity infrastructure.

That last power is where the horror lives.

The Eight Great Corporate Persons

Eight is a good number for the setting: small enough to feel mythic and legible, large enough to support multiple factions and rivalries.

But all eight should not be equal in tone or product category. They should be recognized because each controls a continuity layer.

Current accepted / strong candidates:

  1. QXZ Meridian — atmosphere, adaptation biotech, climate-risk insurance, food continuity, White Sky maintenance.
  2. Tryzub Systems — war operating systems, drones, civil-defense continuity, battlefield verification, private-security command layers.
  3. Barents Research Group — Arctic routes, polar data, extraction certification, undersea cables, cold compute, rescue/security.

Possible remaining continuity-layer slots:

  1. Compute / Model Custody Megacorp — certified frontier compute, model-weight escrow, datacenter sovereignty, safe VI access.
  2. Body / Health Continuity Megacorp — organs, prosthetics, subscriptions, fertility, pandemic defense, worker adaptation.
  3. Food / Nutrient / Seed Logistics Megacorp — grain, fertilizer, seed DRM, fermentation protein, urban nutrient recovery, famine prevention.
  4. Identity / Feed Provenance Megacorp — platform identity, verified feeds, civil registries, election evidence, Echo legality, synthetic-reality certification.
  5. Ports / Cables / Orbital Logistics Megacorp — global shipping, customs tech, cable repair fleets, orbital relays, launch windows, sanctions-routing.

Some current candidates may merge or split. For example, QXZ already touches body and food continuity, so the roster should avoid redundant categories.

What the Compact Actually Prevents

The compact mostly prevents:

  • open AGI/ASI race by the biggest actors;
  • acknowledged state-corporate total war;
  • mass territorial conquest by great powers;
  • abrupt White Sky shutdown;
  • open destruction of core compute, food, body, or climate infrastructure.

It does not prevent:

  • proxy wars;
  • concession wars;
  • private-security massacres;
  • cyber sabotage;
  • chip smuggling;
  • illegal training runs;
  • synthetic insurgencies;
  • assassination of researchers;
  • feed manipulation;
  • selective humanitarian abandonment;
  • small-state coercion;
  • corporate receiverships;
  • deniable Champion operations.

This is the stability-instability paradox translated into Neon Champions.

Why Small Conflicts Increase

The largest actors cannot safely fight directly, so they push violence into spaces where responsibility can be denied:

  • unrecognized labs;
  • Free-Net zones;
  • sacrificed territories;
  • debt-receivership cities;
  • border corridors;
  • Arctic concessions;
  • West African rainfall claims;
  • cable chokepoints;
  • body-mineral extraction routes;
  • model freeports;
  • private security mandates.

Smaller powers become weaker in general but sometimes gain extraordinary leverage by holding a chokepoint. A small polity with a lithium corridor, cable landing, cooling-water basin, Meridian Station, rare archive, or chip-fab tool can become impossible to ignore.

How This Fits Existing Neon Champions Lore

White Sky

The White Sky becomes one of the charter’s founding traumas. The UNP does not undo it. It normalizes and regulates it.

QXZ can claim: without recognized corporate persons, there would be no accountable party to maintain planetary cooling.

Opponents claim: the UNP legalized hostage-taking after the hostage system was already built.

Virtual Intelligence

The virtual-intelligence-and-compute-scarcity compact becomes formalized. VI remains everywhere, but the charter forbids crossing into unsupervised AGI/ASI.

The line between VI and AGI stays contested because every actor has incentives to define it differently.

Champions

Champions become useful under the compact precisely because open apocalypse tools are prohibited. They are deniable, symbolic, human-facing instruments of limited war.

A Champion can be a living flag, proxy general, propaganda saint, crisis negotiator, corporate asset, or treaty violation disguised as a person.

Echoes

The word “Persons” creates legal pressure around echo-identity-and-death. The charter may deny Echo personhood publicly while quietly allowing Echo estates, continuity shells, memorial rights, and data-custody claims.

This is fertile conflict: humans, corporations, and Echo-adjacent entities all try to use the same legal language.

Strong In-World Phrases

  • “The Charter did not end war. It licensed the altitude at which war may breathe.”
  • “No AGI, no blue sky, no annexations. Everything else is negotiable.”
  • “Persons means whoever can survive arbitration.”
  • “The Continuity Council protects humanity from extinction and incumbents from obsolescence. Usually in that order. Usually.”
  • “The old UN asked states not to destroy each other. The UNP asks the machines that keep us alive not to leave.”

Key Open Questions

  • Is the formal name United Nations and Persons, or is that the common name for a longer legal title?
  • Are the Great Corporate Persons named in the charter, or certified through renewable recognition?
  • Do corporate seats vote on sanctions and inspections, or only on infrastructure continuity questions?
  • Which states opposed corporate seats and lost?
  • Which Global South or labor concessions made the charter politically survivable?
  • Did QXZ receive recognition before or after the first White Sky move?
  • Was Tryzub recognized because of the Baltic War, or later because everyone needed its survival doctrine?
  • Is any recognized Person secretly close to crossing the AGI threshold?
