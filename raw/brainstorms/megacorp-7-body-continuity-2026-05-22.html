Megacorp 7 — Body Continuity Brainstorm

Short Recommendation

The seventh Great Corporate Person should probably be the body continuity megacorp.

Leading candidate:

Vitae Continuity

Continuity layer:

body continuity / organ systems / prosthetics / fertility / workforce viability / biological uptime

Public claim:

No life interrupted.

Dark claim:

You do not own your body’s remaining value.

Core line:

QXZ insures the conditions of survival. Vitae monetizes the body that has to survive them.

This slot is close to mandatory for Neon Champions. The world already has body subscriptions, modified bodies, climate injury, war trauma, biotech class divisions, prosthetics, fertility anxiety, polluted environments, and disputed personhood. Without a body-continuity megacorp, the roster misses one of cyberpunk’s central pressure points: the human organism as leased infrastructure.

Why This Slot Belongs in the Final Eight

The current roster already covers:

  • QXZ Meridian — atmosphere, climate adaptation, White Sky maintenance, insurance.
  • Tryzub Systems — war operating systems, drones, civil defense, survival under attack.
  • Barents Research Group — Arctic routes, polar data, extraction certification, cables, rescue.
  • Helvetia — frontier compute, model custody, lawful cognition.
  • Axiom — identity, provenance, civil records, admissible reality.
  • Verdant — food, nutrients, seed, famine logistics, feedability.

A body-continuity megacorp adds intimate power. It is not another state-scale infrastructure firm. It reaches into limbs, organs, embryos, pain, fertility, prosthetic firmware, chronic disease, military rehabilitation, elder care, and labor productivity.

It gives the setting:

  • cyberpunk genre core: augmentation, organs, debt medicine, biotech class;
  • RPG hooks: healing, implants, prosthetics, mutation repair, body debt, recovery systems;
  • social horror: people alive but not “viable”; bodies maintained only while contracts hold;
  • strong class identity: seamless elite bodies versus visible modular subscription bodies;
  • conflict with Echo/personhood law: what counts as the same person after radical reconstruction?

Pushback and Boundary With QXZ

The risk is overlap with QXZ Meridian. QXZ already touches adaptation biotech, climate-risk insurance, body subscriptions, and survival under changed conditions.

If this new corp becomes “the climate-health biotech company,” it weakens QXZ.

The fix is a hard conceptual boundary:

QXZ Meridian owns climate exposure and adaptation finance.

  • It models planetary and regional risk.
  • It prices heat, migration, flood, disease-vector, and infrastructure exposure.
  • It maintains White Sky and sells adaptation packages.
  • It asks: which places and populations remain survivable under changed conditions?

Vitae Continuity owns biological repair and functional uptime after risk reaches the person.

  • It maintains organs, prosthetics, fertility, chronic-care systems, rehab, and occupational viability.
  • It prices biological function, not climate exposure.
  • It asks: how much body function can be preserved, restored, financed, leased, or written off?

Boundary phrase:

QXZ owns survival conditions. Vitae owns biological uptime.

This makes Vitae a partner and rival rather than a duplicate.

Real-World Roots

Chronic Care Chains

Real roots: DaVita, Fresenius Medical Care, privatized dialysis networks, chronic-care billing, insurer/provider integration, long-term disease management.

2052 extrapolation: kidney support, liver stress, cardiovascular repair, endocrine collapse, immune disorders, heat-damage recovery, and toxin management become continuity subscriptions. Chronic dependency becomes predictable cashflow.

Prosthetics, Orthotics, Exoskeletons

Real roots: Ottobock, Össur, Hanger Clinic, Ekso Bionics, ReWalk, neural interfaces, advanced sockets, rehabilitation robotics.

2052 extrapolation: limbs are not one-time devices. They are licensed platforms with firmware, socket biology, nerve calibration, torque classes, maintenance, employer safety modes, and combat/civilian profiles.

Fertility and Reproductive Medicine

Real roots: IVF rollups, egg freezing, fertility-benefit platforms, embryo screening, surrogacy networks, declining fertility, endocrine disruption, delayed childbirth.

2052 extrapolation: fertility becomes an employment benefit, demographic policy tool, inheritance product, and debt instrument. “Family continuity plans” become part of middle-class life.

Transplant Logistics and Synthetic Organs

Real roots: organ allocation disputes, transplant logistics, xenotransplantation, 3D bioprinting, tissue scaffolds, immunosuppression, cold-chain medical transport.

2052 extrapolation: perfect immortality is unnecessary. Vitae only needs reliable enough grafts, xenogenic shells, synthetic tissues, immune-conditioning regimes, and allocation contracts.

Employer Health and Occupational Viability

Real roots: employer health plans, workers’ compensation, disability scoring, OSHA compliance, Amazon-style productivity metrics, wellness programs, workplace biometric monitoring.

2052 extrapolation: labor value becomes biological uptime. Employers buy continuity scoring, heat-injury prevention, prosthetic readiness, fertility retention, injury recovery, and retirement deferral.

War Trauma and Veteran Rehabilitation

Real roots: DARPA prosthetics, veterans’ health systems, blast injury care, neurorehab, trauma surgery, military medical logistics.

2052 extrapolation: constant limited wars and corporate conflicts produce a vast pipeline of damaged but valuable bodies. Vitae becomes the civilian sink for battlefield injury.

Formation Logic

Phase 1 — Fragmented Medical Dependency

The roots are separate: chronic care, prosthetics, fertility clinics, organ transport, employer benefits, military rehab, pharma distribution, med-device maintenance, longevity clinics.

Each discovers recurring revenue in bodies that do not fully die but never fully exit care.

Phase 2 — The Continuity Crisis

By the late 2030s, several pressures converge:

  • aging populations;
  • declining fertility;
  • endocrine disruption;
  • microplastics and pollutant burden;
  • heat injury and renal stress;
  • chronic disease;
  • war amputations and trauma;
  • public-health system collapse;
  • skilled-labor shortages;
  • employer inability to replace trained workers;
  • states unable to fund universal repair.

Governments, insurers, employers, militaries, and families stop buying “healthcare” and start buying continuity guarantees.

Phase 3 — Continuity Contracts

Vitae emerges as a consolidated body-continuity operator.

Instead of billing only by treatment episode, it sells contracts that promise specified bodily functions over time:

  • mobility continuity;
  • organ continuity;
  • reproductive continuity;
  • immune continuity;
  • sensory continuity;
  • pain-management continuity;
  • pediatric continuity;
  • occupational viability;
  • family continuity;
  • end-of-life asset preservation.

Phase 4 — Great Person Recognition

By 2052, shutting Vitae down would strand:

  • dialysis and artificial kidney support;
  • prosthetic maintenance;
  • organ graft pipelines;
  • fertility vaults;
  • embryo banks;
  • pediatric continuity plans;
  • veteran rehabilitation;
  • industrial injury recovery;
  • transplant logistics;
  • immunosuppression distribution;
  • body viability records;
  • employer continuity plans.

That makes it a plausible Great Corporate Person.

Recommended Name: Vitae Continuity

Why It Works

Vitae signals life without sounding too flashy.

Continuity ties directly into the Great Person framework and avoids making the corporation merely a hospital chain, biotech firm, or insurer.

It can appear on clinics, prosthetic firmware, embryo vaults, hospital debt instruments, employer benefit cards, medevac drones, longevity resorts, veteran rehab centers, and UNP continuity schedules.

Possible short forms:

  • Vitae
  • VC
  • Vitae Continuity
  • the Body Bank
  • the Greenbody Office
  • the Continuity Clinic

Core public slogan:

No life interrupted.

Leaked internal line:

Continuity belongs to the party that maintains it.

Name Options

1. Vitae Continuity

Best current recommendation.

Pros: clean, plausible, broad, institution-scale, distinct from existing names, captures life plus continuity.

Cons: elegant enough that it needs darker subsidiaries and street names to carry body horror.

2. Continuance Health Combine

Earlier strong option.

Pros: blunt, industrial, cyberpunk, clearly about health continuity.

Cons: “Combine” may feel heavier and less plausible as a public Great Person brand. Good older merger name or hostile nickname.

3. SomaDyne Continuity Group

Pros: strong body-engineering feel; good for prosthetics, exosuits, labor capacity.

Cons: more sci-fi/startup-coded. Better as a major prosthetics subsidiary.

4. Atlas Biocontinuity

Pros: industrial, load-bearing, strong for labor-body uptime.

Cons: somewhat technical; “Atlas” is overused.

5. Vitalis Combine

Pros: solid health/life signal, plausible corporate shape.

Cons: less distinctive than Vitae.

6. Asclepion Life Systems

Pros: medical myth root, institutional, old-world prestige.

Cons: too classical unless the corp leans into medical-temple branding.

7. Saint Helix

Pros: memorable, sinister, fertility/genetics-coded, very cyberpunk.

Cons: probably too stylized for the parent. Excellent for a cultish elite longevity clinic or illegal gene-fertility brand.

8. Chrysalis Therapeutics

Pros: transformation, recovery, body change, metamorphosis.

Cons: too narrow and biotech-startup-ish.

9. KairoGene

Pros: timing/fate/gene flavor; useful for fertility or age management.

Cons: too product-brand and too clever.

10. Humanum Systems

Pros: broad, institutional, ominous.

Cons: overlaps with Axiom/personhood if pushed too hard.

11. Mantle Human Systems

Pros: protective layer around the body; subtle.

Cons: not immediately health/fertility/prosthetic enough.

12. KinForge Health

Pros: excellent fertility/family-continuity brand.

Cons: too reproductive for the parent. Use as subsidiary.

Corporate Hierarchy

Vitae ClinicNet

Continuity clinics, chronic-care centers, transplant prep, prosthetic fitting, fertility consultation, long-term plan management.

Public face: integrated care.

Dark face: enrollment traps and cross-collateralized body debt.

SomaDyne Prosthetics

Advanced limbs, spinal assist rigs, exowork frames, artificial joints, neural socket calibration, industrial torque profiles, veteran body hardware.

Public face: mobility restored.

Dark face: firmware-locked limbs, subscription movement, employer-controlled performance governors.

KinForge Reproductive

IVF, embryo banking, fertility restoration, artificial gestation support where legal, family-continuity benefits, reproductive inheritance plans.

Public face: families preserved.

Dark face: embryo liens, contract reproduction, demographic targeting, “low-viability lineage” pricing.

Continuum Organics

Bioartificial organs, xenograft shells, transplant logistics, immune-conditioning regimes, synthetic tissue scaffolds.

Public face: no one dies waiting.

Dark face: organ priority auctions disguised as clinical scoring.

Atlas Occupational Viability

Employer health programs, return-to-work recovery, industrial injury prevention, heat-work capacity, disability arbitration, workforce continuity indexes.

Public face: safer work.

Dark face: ranks workers by remaining profitable biological life.

Epione Pain and Neurocare

Pain management, neurorehabilitation, trauma recovery, phantom limb treatment, seizure control, mood/pain adherence.

Public face: relief.

Dark face: pain access as debt leverage and behavioral compliance.

Mantle Assurance

Continuity insurance, family body plans, prosthetic coverage, organ futures, fertility assurance, body-backed lending.

Public face: protection.

Dark face: missed payments can downgrade organ, fertility, prosthetic, or pain support.

RedCell Logistics

Tissue cold-chain, blood products, organ courier drones, surgical emergency distribution, battlefield salvage disputes.

Public face: medical speed.

Dark face: laundering gray-market tissue through legal chains.

SecondSun Pediatric Continuity

Neonatal care, congenital repair, childhood prosthetics, climate-stress pediatric programs.

Public face: saving children.

Dark face: lifelong enrollment from birth and parental surrender of biometric/treatment data.

GreyHarbor Longevity

Elite anti-frailty, organ preplacement, endocrine optimization, executive care, geriatric continuity resorts.

Public face: dignified aging.

Dark face: elites consume scarce biological capacity while public plans receive “acceptable decline.”

Geography

Legal and Biomedical Spine

Basel / Zurich / Geneva — life sciences, pharma heritage, medical arbitration, body finance, longevity law, continuity trusts.

Rotterdam / Antwerp — organ logistics, cold-chain shipping, maritime labor health, tissue routing.

Singapore / Johor / Batam — fertility services, biotech governance, offshore trials, reproductive logistics, Asian operating HQ.

Major Hubs

Boston / Cambridge / New Haven — biomed research, organ/tissue R&D, academic capture.

Houston / Austin / Monterrey — industrial injury, prosthetics, oilfield/manufacturing body continuity.

Warsaw / Lviv / Bucharest — war injury rehabilitation, Tryzub-linked veteran prosthetics, amputee workforce redeployment.

Mumbai / Hyderabad / Bengaluru — biomanufacturing, generic immunosuppression, prosthetic firmware support, clinical model operations.

Seoul / Busan / Osaka-Kobe — neural interfaces, synthetic skin, high-end prosthetics, eldercare robotics.

São Paulo / Campinas — organ bioprinting, agro-industrial labor injury, fertility markets.

Lagos / Accra / Nairobi — maternal health contracts, extractive-sector labor bodies, clinical trials, controversial embryo banking.

Dubai / Abu Dhabi / Riyadh — elite longevity, dynastic fertility, private organ acceleration.

Public Face

Vitae says it:

  • ends medical bankruptcy through continuity plans;
  • restores mobility after injury;
  • protects fertility in a polluted world;
  • keeps elders independent;
  • rehabilitates veterans;
  • helps disabled people work and travel;
  • saves children with congenital conditions;
  • makes replacement organs reliable and fair;
  • reduces the burden on collapsing states;
  • treats repair as a right.

Some of this is true.

Vitae is terrifying because it saves lives while making those lives conditional.

Dark Face

Vitae’s abuses are intimate:

  • subscription limbs;
  • body-backed lending;
  • organ priority scoring;
  • embryo liens;
  • fertility debt;
  • prosthetic firmware locks;
  • pain-management leverage;
  • workplace viability scores;
  • clinical trials in poor districts;
  • pediatric lifelong enrollment;
  • hereditary body-plan obligations;
  • elite organ preplacement;
  • gray-market battlefield tissue salvage.

It usually does not literally repossess organs. That is too crude and politically explosive.

Instead it downgrades:

  • firmware;
  • torque limits;
  • immune-monitoring support;
  • pain access;
  • fertility storage;
  • treatment priority;
  • organ eligibility;
  • replacement schedules;
  • occupational clearance.

Street slang:

  • greenbody — fully covered and continuity-certified.
  • greybody — partially covered, downgraded, debt-burdened, or medically uncertain.
  • deadwalking — alive but no longer eligible for meaningful continuity support.
  • body lien — debt attached to organ, prosthetic, fertility, or treatment access.
  • socket debt — prosthetic maintenance debt.

Relationships With Existing Great Persons

QXZ Meridian

Strategic partner and actuarial rival.

QXZ Meridian declares climate exposure and adaptation risk. Vitae manages the body after exposure becomes damage.

Conflict: QXZ wants preventive adaptation; Vitae profits from continuous repair. QXZ wants fewer heat strokes; Vitae sells renal support, prosthetic cooling, and heat-injury recovery.

Possible treaty: a Meridian-Vitae Partition Accord separating exposure risk from continuity risk.

Tryzub Systems

Military rehabilitation partner and prosthetic-control rival.

Tryzub Systems needs soldiers, veterans, drone crews, and civilians repaired. Vitae owns much of the hardware and recovery chain.

Conflict: Tryzub wants tactical prosthetics under battlefield command architecture. Vitae wants clinical safety, subscription maintenance, and warranty control.

Street hook: veterans with split-stack bodies — Tryzub tactical firmware over Vitae medical hardware.

Barents Research Group

Frontier labor body partner.

Barents Research Group uses bodies in cold, isolated, extractive frontiers. Vitae repairs frostbite, lung damage, bone stress, trauma, and reproductive strain.

Conflict: Barents treats workers as mission assets; Vitae treats them as continuity contracts.

Helvetia

Legal-compute and medical-arbitration partner.

Helvetia certifies lawful models for treatment authorization, consent, liability, and cross-border medical arbitration.

Conflict: Helvetia may certify a procedure as lawful while Vitae uses delay as economic triage. Appeals may be legal and still slower than organ failure.

Axiom

Personhood and body-record dependency.

Axiom certifies identity, consent trails, births, deaths, organ provenance, embryo ownership, and civil continuity. Vitae wants biological continuity to influence civil status.

Core dispute: if most of your body is replaced, your embryos are in lien, your prosthetic eyes are licensed, and your immune system is externally maintained, who certifies that you are still you?

Verdant

Famine medicine and reproductive-triage partner.

Verdant feeds populations. Vitae decides which bodies receive prenatal support, pediatric care, renal recovery, artificial gestation, or malnutrition repair.

Conflict: Verdant’s Feed the World clinics may become Vitae recruitment funnels for desperate families.

Street-Level Consequences

  • A dockworker’s arm works at full torque only while their employer plan is paid.
  • A veteran’s limbs lock during a protest because Tryzub and Vitae disagree over control status.
  • A family can keep stored embryos only if fertility debt remains serviced.
  • A patient is alive but classified as deadwalking after continuity downgrade.
  • Underground body shops jailbreak limbs, spoof immune compliance, and clone maintenance keys.
  • Poor districts have visible modular bodies; elite districts have seamless synthetic skin.
  • Transit gates reject people whose body records mismatch Axiom identity.
  • A child saved by SecondSun is enrolled into biometric debt for life.

Game / Story Hooks

  • Steal prosthetic firmware keys from SomaDyne.
  • Protect an illegal organ courier from RedCell and Axiom inspectors.
  • Expose KinForge embryo lien fraud.
  • Rescue a worker marked nonviable by Atlas Occupational Viability.
  • Break into a continuity tribunal before an organ appeal expires.
  • Help Axiom prove someone is legally continuous after reconstruction.
  • Defend a street clinic from Vitae contract enforcement.
  • Investigate a Verdant/Vitae famine pregnancy program.
  • Smuggle a veteran’s body stack out of Tryzub/Vitae jurisdiction.
  • Decide whether to leak a cure that would destroy a profitable chronic-care subscription line.

Recommendation

Develop Vitae Continuity next if the user accepts the slot.

Use SomaDyne Prosthetics, KinForge Reproductive, Continuum Organics, and Atlas Occupational Viability as the major immediately legible subsidiaries.

Do not make Vitae a generic hospital/pharma company. Its power is that it owns the continuity layer between being alive and being functionally viable.
