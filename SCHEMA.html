Neon Champions Worldbuilding Wiki Schema

Domain

This Obsidian vault describes the lore, setting, world rules, factions, technologies, histories, myths, conflicts, mysteries, and player-facing worldbuilding of Neon Champions.

It supports two goals:

  1. Lore depth: build a coherent, strange, compelling world that exists beyond immediate gameplay needs.
  2. Game bridge: selectively translate lore into factions, units, maps, campaign structure, mechanics, UI language, and player fantasy.

The vault must preserve creative ambiguity without letting the setting become incoherent.

Operating Model

The vault is the living world. It may contain drafts, myths, contradictions, rejected ideas, and trope experiments.

The future game-repo mirror under design/world/ should contain only approved or stable production-facing lore and design bridges.

Canon, world-rule, technology, faction, culture, location, character, organization, conflict, and mystery pages should primarily read as in-universe setting material. They may use frontmatter for canon/status metadata, but the body should avoid real-world research citations, agent instructions, and design notes unless those are clearly separated.

Use this separation:

  • raw/references/ for real-world research notes and source summaries.
  • trope-studies/ for out-of-universe pattern analysis.
  • design-bridges/ for gameplay/mechanics translation.
  • 00-start-here/ audit or planning pages for agent-facing rewrite notes.
  • canon/lore pages for what is true, believed, seen, argued, feared, bought, enforced, or mythologized inside the world.

If a canon page starts accumulating broad neighboring concepts, split or link them rather than letting the page become a general setting essay.

Canon Levels

Every non-raw page must declare one canon level:

  • hard-canon: true unless explicitly revised.
  • soft-canon: currently accepted but open to revision.
  • in-world-belief: believed by a faction, culture, or character; may be false.
  • rumor: unreliable, mythic, propaganda, or contradictory.
  • reference: external inspiration or trope study; not canon.
  • rejected: explicitly not part of Neon Champions.
  • game-bridge: design translation from lore into playable systems.

Status Levels

Every non-raw page must declare one status:

  • seed: rough idea.
  • draft: coherent but not reviewed.
  • reviewed: discussed and internally consistent.
  • approved: accepted as production-facing canon or design guidance.
  • deprecated: superseded, preserved for history.

Naming Conventions

  • Lowercase filenames.
  • Hyphenated names.
  • No spaces.
  • Singular nouns unless the concept is naturally plural.

Examples:

  • factions/chrome-saints.md
  • technologies/echo-resurrection.md
  • canon/world-rules/digital-net.md
  • trope-studies/rogue-ai-gods.md
  • mysteries/first-champion.md

Cross-Linking Rules

Every non-raw page should link to at least two other pages using Obsidian wikilinks.

Example:

Pages should link both conceptually and practically:

  • faction → technologies it uses
  • faction → enemies
  • technology → world rule it depends on
  • mystery → conflicting beliefs
  • trope study → canon pages it influenced
  • canon page → design bridge if gameplay-relevant

Raw Source Rules

Files under raw/ are immutable after ingestion.

Use raw/ for:

  • TV Tropes notes
  • copied excerpts
  • research notes
  • brainstorm transcripts
  • reference summaries
  • images and assets

Raw files are not canon.

Update Policy

When new lore conflicts with existing content:

  1. Do not silently overwrite.
  2. Check whether the conflict is canon contradiction, in-world disagreement, rumor, or old draft residue.
  3. If useful, create or update a page in contradictions/.
  4. Mark contested pages with contested: true and contradictions: [].
  5. Ask for human canon decision before promoting conflicting material to approved.

Mirror Policy

Only mirror pages to the game repo when:

  • status: approved or explicitly selected by the user.
  • game_relevance is medium, high, or core.
  • The page has a clear design bridge or production-facing summary.

The vault remains exploratory. The mirror remains stable.
