Player-Facing Design Premise

The Digital-Net should become the strategic map’s information war layer: scouting, fog-of-war, misinformation, provenance, feed manipulation, targeted accounting, and event delivery.

The map is not hidden only by distance. It is hidden by dirty information.

The core player question:

Do you act quickly on polluted information, spend time to verify it, weaponize it as propaganda, suppress it, or risk being deceived?

This is not cyber-magic. The effects should come from sensors, feeds, relays, archives, credentials, VI prediction, public channels, Axiom provenance, Chorus amplification, Free-Net leaks, and degraded infrastructure.

Design Role

Primary Role: Fog-of-War and Scouting System

Digital-Net systems define what the player knows about the strategic map:

  • visible terrain and sites;
  • rumored objectives;
  • enemy movement reports;
  • climate hazard warnings;
  • settlement unrest;
  • hidden routes;
  • station status;
  • false contacts;
  • stale data;
  • provenance of claims.

Secondary Role: Trust and Provenance Layer

The player should often know something before knowing whether it is reliable.

Useful distinctions:

  • true but not admissible;
  • legally certified but misleading;
  • socially viral but false;
  • locally witnessed but untrusted;
  • Free-Net leaked but contaminated;
  • Chorus-amplified but politically weaponized;
  • Axiom-certified but institutionally captured.

Tertiary Role: Campaign Event Engine

Polluted feeds generate events that are not just flavor:

  • scandal packets;
  • false flags;
  • Champion myth surges;
  • Echo distress signals;
  • climate dashboard panic;
  • targeted-accounting lockouts;
  • feed riots;
  • public bounties;
  • fake routes;
  • suppressed proof;
  • Blue Break attribution wars.

Core Map Information Layers

The player should be able to reason about several information layers, even if the first prototype exposes only a few.

LayerMeaningGameplay Use
VisibilityWhat units/sites/terrain are physically visible.Classic exploration and tactical certainty.
SignalWhat sensors, feeds, drones, scouts, relays, or archives claim exists.Early warning, rumors, false contacts.
ProvenanceWhy the player should or should not trust a report.Verification, diplomacy, legal access, scandal defense.
AttentionWhere the public is watching, panicking, donating, recruiting, or spreading rumors.Morale, unrest, bounties, recruitment, Champion fame.
AccountingHow bodies, movement, risk, cooling, insurance, clinic access, and faction trust are priced.Travel costs, upkeep, policing risk, bodyware access.

Design rule:

Physical scouting should remain the gold standard for tactical certainty. Remote data is faster, broader, and politically powerful, but often stale, partial, or manipulated.

Strategic Map Sites

Digital-Net sites should function like HoMM3 adventure-map objects translated into cyberpunk infrastructure.

Relay Mast

Expands signal and scouting radius.

Possible effects:

  • reveals nearby movement reports;
  • improves remote command range;
  • enables drone scouting;
  • can be jammed, spoofed, or captured.

Civic Sensor Hub

Municipal or regional sensor system for traffic, weather, disease, water, movement, unrest, and infrastructure.

Possible effects:

  • reveals hazard clocks;
  • shows city unrest;
  • detects convoys;
  • creates proof if records are preserved.

Free-Net Archive

Cracked archive, abandoned mirror, municipal leak, academic cache, pirate climate model, old satellite store, or dead platform.

Possible effects:

  • reveals hidden routes;
  • exposes suppressed history;
  • provides climate or casualty evidence;
  • unlocks Champion/Echo hooks;
  • may be stale, trapped, edited, or a honeypot.

Polluted Feed Tower

A high-volume media, advertising, alert, or faction-feed node.

Possible effects:

  • creates attention pressure;
  • spreads propaganda;
  • boosts or damages morale;
  • generates false contacts;
  • triggers panic during climate events.

Axiom Proof Node

Registry, evidence, identity, or provenance facility tied to axiom.

Possible effects:

  • converts verified information into admissible proof;
  • unlocks contracts, tribunals, migration permissions, city access;
  • can launder institutional truth while excluding unofficial witnesses.

Chorus Resonance Hub

Media, creator, payment, remittance, ranking, or social-amplification site tied to chorus-exchange.

Possible effects:

  • amplifies discoveries;
  • boosts recruitment, donations, and public morale;
  • spreads scandals, bounties, and Champion myths;
  • can make a false event strategically real.

Dead Server / Ghost Farm

Old server cluster, abandoned model farm, battlefield archive, or memorial platform.

Possible effects:

  • supplies old data, Echo traces, ghost contacts, or hidden map history;
  • risks grief loops, malware, VI lures, and false memories.

Black-Market Mirror

Temporary illegal access to cleaner or simply alternate data.

Possible effects:

  • reveals unregistered settlements, illegal clinics, hidden movements, or suppressed maps;
  • tags the player with legal exposure or targeted accounting risk.

Targeting Ledger

Regional accounting system that prices movement, bodies, insurance, cooling, policing, work permits, bounty exposure, and faction trust.

Possible effects:

  • increases enemy policing efficiency;
  • raises travel/upkeep costs;
  • locks units out of clinics or transit;
  • can be seized, spoofed, exposed, erased, or nationalized.

Spoofing / Jamming Node

Hostile or improvised information-warfare site.

Possible effects:

  • creates false armies;
  • hides real sites;
  • distorts route data;
  • slows reinforcements;
  • corrupts hazard warnings.

Core Mechanics

1. Scouting with Confidence

Map reports should carry metadata:

FieldPurpose
SourceScout, sensor, Free-Net, Axiom, Chorus, faction, rumor, captured ledger.
AgeHow many turns since the information was observed.
ConfidenceHow likely the report is to be current and accurate.
ProvenanceWhether it is local, verified, admissible, disputed, or anonymous.
PollutionClean, noisy, spoofed, stale, ghost data, or honeypot.

Example reports:

  • Enemy convoy: 72% confidence, Chorus chatter, 2 turns old, polluted.
  • Meridian anomaly: Axiom-sealed, 95% confidence, admissible.
  • Hidden route: Free-Net archive, 50% confidence, possible honeypot.
  • Relief riot: locally witnessed, no Axiom recognition, high attention.

2. Verification as a Strategic Action

Verification should cost time, movement, compute, attention, risk, or exposure.

Possible progression:

Tools that can verify:

  • scouts;
  • Champions with Reach or Interface;
  • drones;
  • captured relays;
  • Axiom nodes;
  • sensor fortresses;
  • Free-Net archivists;
  • local witnesses;
  • Meridian calibration sites.

Verification should be valuable because it can change diplomacy, city access, scandal outcomes, tribunal results, and public legitimacy.

3. Misinformation as Terrain

Polluted feed zones act like information terrain.

Possible penalties:

  • false enemy stacks;
  • hidden site ownership;
  • wrong hazard-clock estimates;
  • delayed crisis warnings;
  • distorted faction reputation;
  • fake morale reports;
  • route mispricing;
  • incorrect station-state displays.

Counterplay is mandatory. False information should be patterned by source, region, faction, or site. If every report may be random lies, players will stop caring.

4. Targeted Accounting

Targeted accounting turns surveillance into material pressure.

Regional effects can include:

  • travel fees;
  • checkpoint risk;
  • clinic access;
  • bodyware maintenance costs;
  • cooling priority;
  • migration permits;
  • policing intensity;
  • public bounty exposure;
  • city contract eligibility;
  • faction trust;
  • Champion legitimacy.

Player actions:

  • spoof a ledger;
  • expose ledger abuse;
  • seize a ledger node;
  • erase a Champion from pricing systems;
  • make a settlement unpriceable;
  • accept clean corporate accounting for stability;
  • trade privacy for clinic/cooling access.

5. Provenance as a Strategic Resource

A possible campaign resource: Proof.

Proof is not identical to truth. It is information that can survive institutions.

Uses:

  • prove climate harm;
  • defend a Champion scandal;
  • unlock city gates, contracts, or migration corridors;
  • counter a false flag;
  • legitimize station capture;
  • trigger sanctions or compensation;
  • expose corporate emergency powers.

Axiom improves admissibility. Free-Net improves access to suppressed claims. Chorus improves attention. None of them guarantees moral truth.

6. Attention as Pressure

Chorus-style attention decides whether information becomes socially active.

A true event may be ignored without attention. A false event may become strategically real if amplified.

Effects:

  • recruitment spikes;
  • unrest;
  • surrender pressure;
  • scandal damage;
  • public bounties;
  • donation surges;
  • panic during Blue Breaks;
  • Champion fame, martyrdom, or delegitimization.

7. Free-Net Access

Free-Net access is not truth mode.

It can provide:

  • old satellite data;
  • municipal leaks;
  • hidden routes;
  • climate records;
  • unregistered settlements;
  • suppressed casualties;
  • unaudited Champion footage;
  • illegal clinic maps;
  • pre-White-Sky media.

It can also contain:

  • traps;
  • outdated records;
  • faction edits;
  • honeypots;
  • hostile VI fragments;
  • Echo grief loops;
  • fake coordinates;
  • legal exposure.

Polluted Feed Event Seeds

False Convoy

A feed shows enemy movement. It may be a real convoy, a decoy, stale drone data, or a baited ambush.

Ghost Route

An old map reveals a shortcut. It may be collapsed, mined, inhabited, flooded, or real.

Provenance Challenge

A rescue, atrocity, or battle victory is downgraded to “unverified synthetic event.” The player needs proof or attention.

Scandal Packet

A Champion’s legitimacy is attacked through leaked, forged, or ambiguously real evidence.

Civic Alert Collapse

A city loses trusted alerts during a climate hazard, siege, or station failure.

Ledger Lock

A Champion or army loses access to clinics, cooling, pay, transit, or supplies because targeted accounting flags them.

Feed Panic

A polluted climate dashboard triggers unrest, evacuation, or hoarding.

Clean Mirror

Temporary access to unusually reliable map data. It may be a gift, an accident, or a trap.

Honeypot Archive

A Free-Net node offers powerful data but tags the player, exposes their scouts, or injects false route data.

Echo Distress Signal

A signal may be a dead Champion trace, grief product, VI lure, faction hoax, or true continuity mystery.

Champion Interactions

Tie information systems directly to champions-design-bridge.

Champion AxisDigital-Net Use
ReachExpands relay/scouting radius; operates through degraded networks.
InterfaceResists spoofing, uses Free-Net, controls drones/sensors, manipulates ledgers.
LegitimacyHelps discoveries become socially or legally actionable.
PresenceTurns information into morale, recruitment, panic, surrender, or scandal.
CommandReduces army penalties in polluted, spoofed, or dead-zone environments.

Candidate Champion archetypes:

  • Free-Net Warden: scout, archive guide, deception-resistant dead-zone commander.
  • War-OS Commander: battlefield mesh expert; strong spoof resistance but license-dependent.
  • Chorus Idol-Commander: attention-war specialist; vulnerable to scandal.
  • Echo-Bound Veteran: detects ghost data, battlefield records, and memorial archive anomalies.
  • Corporate Stabilization Champion: converts sensor data into contracts and legitimacy; vulnerable to Free-Net exposure.
  • Low-Signal Warlord: thrives where ledgers fail and physical presence beats feeds.

Interaction with White Sky / Meridian Stations

The white-sky-and-meridian-stations bridge already gives Meridian Stations a resource called Sensor Truth. Digital-Net systems determine whether Sensor Truth is visible, trusted, admissible, or weaponized.

Strong combined uses:

  • Meridian sensor fortresses become information objectives.
  • Climate dashboards become fog-of-war sources.
  • Blue Break events trigger feed panic and attribution warfare.
  • Free-Net leaks contradict QXZ station reports.
  • Axiom proof nodes determine whether sabotage, negligence, or natural variance is legally recognized.
  • Chorus hubs determine whether the public sees a Blue Break as miracle, terrorism, liberation, fraud, or proof of corporate failure.

White Sky status overlays can exist in several versions at once:

  • true physical state;
  • QXZ public dashboard;
  • local witness reports;
  • Free-Net leaked model;
  • Axiom-certified record;
  • Chorus-amplified narrative;
  • faction propaganda claim.

UI / Naming / Visual Language

Fog should not be only black.

Suggested visual states:

  • dark grey: unknown;
  • amber: rumored;
  • pale blue-white: Axiom-certified;
  • dirty green: Free-Net;
  • neon magenta/cyan: Chorus-amplified;
  • static/noise: polluted;
  • red glitch: spoofed or honeypot;
  • flat blank: dead zone;
  • translucent afterimage: ghost data.

Reports should expose enough metadata for fair play:

  • source;
  • age;
  • confidence;
  • provenance;
  • pollution state;
  • possible manipulation vector;
  • available verification actions.

Prototype caution: start with a small number of readable states. Suggested first set:

Minimal Prototype Shape

First playable test:

  • one region;
  • one relay mast;
  • one Free-Net archive;
  • one polluted feed tower;
  • one Axiom proof node;
  • one Meridian sensor site;
  • one enemy army partly hidden by spoofing;
  • one Champion with Reach or Interface advantage.

Player choice loop:

  1. Receive several reports with different confidence/provenance.
  2. Decide whether to move, scout, verify, amplify, suppress, or exploit.
  3. Face a climate/station crisis where bad information can cause wrong action.
  4. Use proof or attention to shape the aftermath.

Risks

Player Frustration

False information can feel like the designer lying.

Mitigation: show source, age, confidence, and warning states; provide counterplay.

UI Overload

Too many overlays can bury the HoMM3-style strategic loop.

Mitigation: scenarios foreground only a few states; advanced overlays unlock later.

Random Noise

If everything may be false, nothing matters.

Mitigation: make deception patterned by faction, site, source, and region.

Provenance Confused with Truth

Axiom-certified should mean institutionally admissible, not metaphysically true.

Mitigation: keep “truth,” “proof,” and “attention” distinct.

Free-Net Romanticization

Free-Net should reveal suppressed truths but also contain rot, traps, edits, myths, and grief loops.

Cyber-Magic Drift

Avoid instant hacking powers.

Mitigation: ground abilities in relays, access, credentials, compute, social reach, and physical scouting.

Undermining Exploration

If remote data reveals too much, map movement weakens.

Mitigation: remote reports decay; physical scouting remains best for tactical certainty.

Decisions Needed

  1. How false can map data be?

    • Recommendation: allow false contacts, but make them source-tagged and counterable.

  2. Is “verified” always true?

    • Recommendation: no. Split sensor-verified, Axiom-admissible, socially believed, and actually true.

  3. Should Proof be a visible strategic resource?

    • Recommendation: yes, at least at campaign layer.

  4. How deep should targeted accounting be in the first prototype?

    • Recommendation: start as regional modifiers and events: travel fees, clinic access, bounty exposure, contract access.

  5. Can players deliberately pollute feeds?

    • Recommendation: yes, with legitimacy and blowback risk.

  6. Is Free-Net access universal or faction-specific?

    • Recommendation: both. Anyone can find fragments; Free-Net-aligned Champions and factions use them better.

  7. How much Axiom/Chorus complexity should appear early?

    • Recommendation: include functions first: Axiom = admissible proof; Chorus = amplification/attention.

  8. Should dead zones be penalties or opportunities?

    • Recommendation: both. They weaken dashboards and ledgers but empower physical scouting, local guides, and low-signal factions.
